About Me

I am Josephine Grace P. Labador, often called “Sefie”— derived from JoSEPHIne. My parents believed that changing PH with F would sound fancier. I was born on March 7, 2011 so I’m now 14 years old. I wouldn’t say I have just one set of hobbies well I consider myself a jack of all trades, a master of none. While I’m drawn most strongly to anything artistic, I find inspiration in all kinds of creative expression. After all, isn’t everything a form of art in its own way? In short, I’m passionate about exploring, creating, and appreciating a little bit of everything.

One fact about me is I don’t really have a favorite place. I don’t care where I am—what matters is who I’m with. Honestly, my biggest blessing is my friends and the people around me. Toxic relationships? Nope, not in my dictionary. I always try to show love and care, and they do the same for me (which is super awesome). I love them so much it feels like my heart could explode sometimes. Because of them, I barely feel sad—maybe just once in a blue moon. Most of the time, I’m just happy. Sure, we all make mistakes and sometimes things get messy, but they’re always there for me, and I’m so thankful. Sometimes I cry when I think about the hard times we went through together and how we never left each other. And now I’m literally tearing up again because I love them so much.

I could pen a thousand words yet still fall short of what’s inside in writing about how much I love my family. “You do not choose your family, GOD chooses them for you.” I’ve heard from my parents. That quote hit me and made me thank God sooo much for giving me this wonderful family. I am so blessed that I consider my family perfect, in all life aspects. I could describe my family but it will be too much. All I can say is that much like a tree, the Foundation of my being is deeply rooted in the lessons and experiences that is nourished by the people I mostly Trust and Love. Hence, the knowledge and actions I exercise in my life is mostly influenced by them.
