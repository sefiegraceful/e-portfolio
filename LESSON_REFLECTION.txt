    <h2>Lesson 1 — What is CSS?</h2>
    <p>
      I learned that CSS, or Cascading Style Sheets, is what gives life to a webpage’s appearance. It separates content from design, making websites easier to maintain and more visually appealing. CSS controls layout, color, and spacing — allowing one file to style multiple pages consistently. This taught me how structure (HTML) and style (CSS) work together to create an organized and attractive site.
    </p>

  <div class="card">
    <h2>Lesson 2 — Style Sheets: Structure & Types</h2>
    <p>
      This lesson helped me understand the anatomy of CSS rules — the selector, property, and value — and how each part defines a webpage’s design. I also learned about the three types of CSS: external, internal, and inline. Each one serves a different purpose: external for overall site control, internal for page-specific styles, and inline for small changes. It made me realize the importance of keeping code neat and consistent.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <h2>Lesson 3 — Selectors & Properties</h2>
    <p>
      Here I explored how selectors target specific elements and how properties determine what those elements look like. I learned that using class, ID, and universal selectors helps manage large websites more efficiently. Understanding properties like <code>color</code>, <code>font-size</code>, and <code>margin</code> gave me the power to control design details. It taught me precision — that even small CSS rules can completely transform a page’s look.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <h2>Lesson 4 — Colors, Fonts, & Text</h2>
    <p>
      This lesson focused on typography and color — two of the most expressive parts of web design. I learned how to use RGB, HEX, and HSL color codes, as well as font families, weights, and sizes to create readable and stylish text. I also discovered text effects like shadows and spacing that improve the user’s visual experience. It showed me how design can set the tone and personality of a website.
    </p>
